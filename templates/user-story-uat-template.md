## Acceptance criteria
Write criteria in a testable format:

Include positive, negative, boundary, authorization, duplicate-submission, notification, and failure scenarios where relevant.

## Sign-off checklist
- [ ] Business rules and scope validated
- [ ] Acceptance criteria reviewed by stakeholders
- [ ] Positive and negative scenarios covered
- [ ] Permissions and data handling considered
- [ ] Notification and failure paths considered
- [ ] UAT evidence recorded
- [ ] Outstanding defects and decisions documented
- [ ] Approval and release decision recorded

**Reminder:** Clearly distinguish confirmed requirements from assumptions and proposed designs.
